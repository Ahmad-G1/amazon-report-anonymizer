# Hide names in your Amazon reports

A free page that swaps the names in your Amazon reports for codes. You can then share the reports without showing your brand or your products.

**Open the page:** https://ahmad-g1.github.io/amazon-report-anonymizer/

## How it works

1. Open the page and add your Amazon reports (CSV, text or Excel).
2. The page swaps your brand, product titles, ASINs, SKUs and order numbers for codes like `SKU-4` and `ASIN-12`. It removes buyer names, emails, phone numbers and addresses.
3. Download the coded files and your key file. Share the coded files. Keep the key.
4. When a report comes back, add your key and the report. The codes turn back into names.

The same product gets the same code in every file, so your reports still fit together.

## Your data stays with you

- Nothing is uploaded. The page runs in your browser and has no server behind it.
- It loads nothing from the internet. To check: open the page, turn off your wifi, then use it.
- You can save the page and run it from your computer. It works the same.
- Only you hold the key. Do not send it to anyone.

## Before you share

Step 3 on the page shows what was done to every column. Read it. No tool catches every name, above all in notes and other free text. Prices, categories and search terms stay readable, so they show what kind of product you sell.

## Good to know

- Download your reports with Seller Central set to English.
- Pictures, PDFs and Word files are not coded. The page leaves them out and says so.
- Tested in Chrome on practice files. Tell me if a report of yours does not work.
